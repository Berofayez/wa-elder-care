# Planning process

We plan every aspect of the product before writing any code. Steps are done
one at a time; a step is only marked done once the founder has approved it.
If a later step reveals a problem with an earlier decision, we come back and
revise the earlier doc rather than letting the docs drift out of sync with
reality.

## Steps

1. **Vision & problem definition** — what problem, for whom, why now
2. **Market & user segments** — who exactly are "clients" and "caregivers"
3. **Business model** — how the business makes money, who pays whom
4. **Trust, safety & compliance** — WA licensing, background checks, insurance, liability
5. **MVP scope** — the smallest version that is actually useful
6. **Core user flows & UX** — key journeys for clients, family members, and caregivers
7. **Information architecture** — entities, data model at a product level
8. **Technical architecture** — stack, hosting, integrations
9. **Launch & roadmap** — phased rollout, v1 vs later
